Welcome to Babil International LLC

An import and export house that grew into one of Oman's most dynamic business groups.

Babil International LLC was established in April 1991 in the Sultanate of Oman. What began with imports from China, India and neighbouring GCC countries now spans suppliers in Germany, Malaysia, Indonesia, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Cyprus, Brazil, Turkey, Italy and Greece, and a group of companies that serves modern Oman with a well-trained, efficient workforce.
