01 · Wholesale

Wholesale Division

Internationally known products, delivered to every corner of Oman

Wholesale Marketing, a division of the Babil group, distributes some of the internationally well-known products in the Oman market through its well-equipped infrastructure.

The division focuses on foodstuff and non-foodstuff categories, offering a range of varieties in each product, and has made a name of its own in the Oman market. A trustworthy name, excellent customer service and reliable delivery were the stepping stones of its success.

A well-appointed team conducts market surveys from time to time to tap the needs of customers and decide on product supply. The secret behind the department's success is the reach of its products, even to the interior areas of the Sultanate, together with competitive prices and the standard of quality maintained across every product.

03 · Special Projects

Special Project Division

A leading supplier to government organisations since 2003

In May 2003 we started this division to look into tender notices from various government institutions. With efficient service, excellent quality and compatible prices, in a short span of time we received contracts from the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Education for the supply of various items.

Our policy of high-quality products at a reasonable price, with proper delivery of goods on time, has made Babil Special Projects one of the major competitors in this business segment. The wealth of experience and excellent customer service it provides ensures that it continues to be a leading supplier to several government organisations in the Sultanate of Oman.
