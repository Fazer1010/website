Imports that began close to home now arrive from three continents.

Babil International LLC was established in April 1991 as an import and export company based in the Sultanate of Oman. The first imports came mainly from China, India and neighbouring GCC countries. The business was later enhanced by establishing imports from many other countries, among them Germany, Malaysia, Indonesia, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Cyprus, Brazil, Turkey, Italy and Greece.

Presently Babil International LLC is a group of companies equipped to cater to the needs of modern Oman through its range of products and services, establishing itself as one of the most dynamic business groups in the Sultanate with a well-trained and efficient workforce. The assiduity of that team propelled the company's growth within a short span of time.

With an annual turnover of 12 million Omani Rials, the Babil group has diversified into several business verticals: Babil Special Projects, retail chains, wholesale marketing, IT services, constructions, and restaurants and catering services. Ably guided under the leadership of Mr. S. M. Basheer, the Managing Director, the company is headquartered in Oman with field operations spread across the Sultanate and other parts of the globe.

Business verticals

Six ways the Babil group serves Oman

Each vertical is run by its own team, sharing the group's infrastructure, supplier network and customer-first standards.

Started in May 2003 to answer tender notices from government institutions, the division supplies clothing, webbing bags, desert boots, safety boots, uniforms, tents, camouflage nets, badges, medals, furniture and much more to the Ministry of Defence, Royal Oman Police, Royal Guard of Oman and other bodies.

Babil Hypermarkets, marts and branded showrooms in Al Khoudh, Mabelah, Sinaw and Mawelah, designed to meet the needs of the modern Omani consumer with fresh fruits and vegetables, grocery, bakery, dairy, beverages and frozen food.

Distributes internationally known foodstuff and non-food products through the Oman market, with a team that surveys customer needs regularly and a reach that extends to the interior of the Sultanate.

Technology services that support the group's own operations and clients in the Sultanate, one of the diverse interests Babil has developed alongside trading.

Construction activity that complements the group's retail expansion and its supply relationships with institutions across Oman.

Hospitality services in the Sultanate, drawing on the same supply chain that keeps Babil's hypermarkets stocked with fresh food every day.
